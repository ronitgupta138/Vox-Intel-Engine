# Lexical & Acoustic Disfluency Grammar Classification Rules

## 1. Detection Taxonomy
- **Filled Pauses:** "uh", "um", "er", "ah" (characterized by low pitch variation and elongated duration $> 200\text{ms}$).
- **Repetitions:** Immediate word duplication (e.g., "the the", "we we").
- **False Starts / Restarts:** Abrupt syntactic abandonment followed by speech restarts.
